## Privacy and safe screenshot rules

- Obscure all real Customer names, employee names, phone numbers, WhatsApp numbers, email addresses and internal draft/record IDs.
- Do not include individual live message contents, confidential price/credit terms, consent audit records, sensitive notes or account passwords.
- Keep the area around the button or status visible so the screenshot still teaches something.
- Remove image metadata before adding a screenshot to a public repository.
- Use a new capture after any material UI change; do not quietly substitute a screenshot from another application version.

## Further administrator captures needed

The provided screenshots cover the principal Dayboard and three reports. They do **not** show the following interfaces in detail:

- **Manager Daily Review**: fallback-owner items, duplicate overrides, recent cancellations, drafts older than two days.
- **Overdue Followups**, **Data Quality Queue**, **Needs Review Queue**, **Duplicate Override Audit** and **Contact Moments Log**.
- **Commercial Config / Commercial Settings** form and permitted field values.
- **Commercial Pipeline**, **Commercial Pipeline Membership**, status transition dialog and owner/contact rules.
- **WhatsApp Template**, **Commercial Contact Outcome**, customer contact-channel permission and a safe demonstration of the full contact-detail panel.
- **Generate Today** dialog, especially Preview versus manager-only destructive modes.
